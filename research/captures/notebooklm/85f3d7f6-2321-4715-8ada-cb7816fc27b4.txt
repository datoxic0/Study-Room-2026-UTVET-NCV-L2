This manual provides a comprehensive study guide for the NCV Level 2 Manual Manufacturing engineering exam, emphasizing the theoretical mechanics of a G-strap clamping device. It covers critical metrology tools like Vernier calipers and outside micrometers, alongside proper layout techniques utilizing Engineers' Blue and various punches. The text outlines hand cutting and forming methods, highlighting the importance of Teeth Per Inch (TPI) selection and accounting for material springback during bending. Additionally, it details internal and external thread-cutting procedures using hand taps and button dies to ensure precise assembly. Finally, the guide stresses essential workshop safety protocols, including personal protective equipment and hazard controls, to prepare students for both theoretical and practical exam questions.
