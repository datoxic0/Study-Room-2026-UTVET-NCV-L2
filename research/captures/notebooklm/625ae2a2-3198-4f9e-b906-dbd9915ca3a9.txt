This document presents an Electrotechnology NQF Level 2 examination paper administered by the South African Department of Higher Education and Training on November 21, 2025. The three-hour assessment is worth 100 marks and is designed to test vocational students on fundamental electrical principles, circuit diagrams, and mathematical calculations. It includes five main questions covering true-or-false statements, schematic symbols, electrical block diagrams, multiple-choice theory, and practical fault-finding scenarios. Additionally, the examination package provides a comprehensive formula sheet with thirty-one mathematical equations alongside an addendum for recording maintenance work reports.
