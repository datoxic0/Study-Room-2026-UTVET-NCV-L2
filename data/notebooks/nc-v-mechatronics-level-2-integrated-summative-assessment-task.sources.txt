Activity 3.1: Planning for the Manufacturing Task (Worksheet 6)
Five Key Work Sequence Processes:
Preparation and Marking: Measure, scribe layout lines, and center-punch reference points on the material according to the engineering drawing specifications[cite: 1, 2].
Material Removal & Squaring: Cut away excess waste material, file surfaces flat and square to the reference marks, and deburr all edges to eliminate sharp points[cite: 1, 2].
Precision Drilling: Prepare the drill press, clamp the work piece securely, and drill the main frame hole for the screw thread as well as the ends of the screw handle[cite: 1, 2].
Internal Thread Fabrication: Tap the main frame hole to the specified thread size and apply a lead-in chamfer for smooth engagement[cite: 1, 2].
Assembly & Finishing: Perform a force-fit assembly of the handle and washer onto the screw thread, complete final fitting, clean tools, and tidy the workstation[cite: 1, 2].
Tool Requirements:
Marking & Layout: Steel rule, scriber, engineer's square, center punch, and ball-peen hammer[cite: 1, 2].
Cutting & Shaping: Hacksaw, flat hand files, square files, and deburring tools[cite: 1, 2].
Drilling & Threading: Drill press, machine vice/clamps, twist drill bits, tap set with tap wrench, and chamfering countersink bit[cite: 1, 2].
Safety Requirements:
Safety goggles are mandatory during all drilling and material cutting operations to protect against high-velocity metal swarf and chips[cite: 1, 2].
Ensure work pieces are clamped securely in vices before starting any drilling or cutting operation[cite: 1, 2].
Maintain clean, hazard-free walkways and keep hands clear of moving machinery[cite: 1, 2].
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Activity 3.2: Manufacturing the Work Piece
Measuring, Scribing, and Center Punching: Verify stock dimensions against the drawing, scribe all reference lines, and mark hole centers with a center punch prior to cutting or drilling[cite: 1, 2].
Rough Cutting and Filing: Cut away excess stock with a hacksaw, file all faces flat and square to reference marks, and deburr all edges[cite: 1, 2].
Drilling Operations: Securely mount the work piece on the drill press and drill the primary screw-thread clearance/tap hole and screw-handle end holes to exact dimensions[cite: 1, 2].
Tapping and Chamfering: Apply a lead-in chamfer to the drilled hole to prevent thread distortion and tap the frame hole to the specified thread size using cutting fluid[cite: 1, 2].
Force-Fit Assembly: Assemble the screw-thread handle at one end and the washer at the other end using a high-precision interference force fit to withstand operational clamping torque[cite: 1, 2].
Housekeeping & Storage: Deburr finished edges, clean all hand and power tools, return tools to the store, and clear the work area[cite: 1, 2].
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Activity 3.3: Knowledge Questionnaire (Worksheet 7)
Question 1 — Purpose of the Lead-in Chamfer: Chamfering the tapped hole creates a tapered entry that facilitates smooth thread engagement, prevents cross-threading, and protects the entrance threads from mechanical damage during heavy clamping loads[cite: 1].
Question 2 — Mechanics of the Force-Fit Assembly: The handle and washer utilize high-precision interference fit tolerances to create a permanent mechanical bond capable of resisting rotational and axial torque during industrial clamping without slippage[cite: 1].
Question 3 — Occupational Health, Safety, and Housekeeping (OHS): Mandatory use of eye protection (safety goggles) prevents ocular injuries from metal swarf[cite: 1, 2]. Immediate cleaning and returning of tools to designated storage prevents workspace clutter, minimizes trip hazards, and protects precision measuring faces from degradation[cite: 1, 2].
