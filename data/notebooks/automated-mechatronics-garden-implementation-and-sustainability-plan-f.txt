The provided materials outline a comprehensive blueprint for developing an Automated Mechatronics Garden in the heat-stressed region of Cwaka, Msinga. This initiative serves as a practical masterclass for students to apply engineering concepts—such as IoT architecture, thermodynamics, and mechanical design—to overcome extreme local food insecurity. Central to the project is a Ground-to-Air Heat Transfer system that leverages stable underground temperatures to cool the greenhouse environment. The technical framework utilizes an ESP32 microcontroller alongside industrial-grade sensors to automate precision irrigation, UV-shielding, and ventilation. Economically, the initiative relies on a sustainable business model where high-value crops are sold to cover operational expenses while providing fresh produce to the local community.
