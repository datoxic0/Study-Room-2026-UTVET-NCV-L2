Activity 2.2: Vero Board Layout, Assembly, and Soldering
Layout Diagram Requirements:
Draw the component layout showing all components clearly labelled[cite: 1].
Mark all soldering points and copper track breaks clearly on the grid template[cite: 1].
Indicate external fly-lead connection points for field devices (push buttons, toggle switch, battery supply, and female chassis plugs)[cite: 1].
Soldering and Assembly Standards:
Track Breaks: Cut cleanly across designated copper strips to prevent short-circuiting parallel components[cite: 1].
Polarity Check: Verify orientation for polarized components (LEDs, electrolytic capacitors, and diodes) before soldering[cite: 1].
Solder Quality: Joints must be shiny, smooth, and concave without dry joints, overheating, or solder bridges between tracks[cite: 1].
Wiring: Strip, tin, and connect fly-leads securely between the vero board and chassis-mounted hardware[cite: 1].
