The provided materials outline a National Certificate Vocational assessment for Mechatronics Level 2 students involving three practical sub-tasks designed to evaluate technical competencies. Sub-task one focuses on computer assembly, peripheral connection, and the creation and simulation of a Programmable Logic Controller program featuring specific start, stop, and timer functions. Sub-task two requires learners to layout, drill, and wire an electronic circuit enclosure, assemble components onto a vero board, and demonstrate proper soldering and safety standards. Sub-task three challenges candidates to manufacture a physical G-clamp from an engineering drawing by measuring, cutting, drilling, and finishing the raw material. Finally, the documentation provides comprehensive assessment checklists and marking grids to measure student performance across all designated practical and theoretical requirements.
